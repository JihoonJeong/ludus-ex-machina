# Forensic Report — Laundry Room Evidence

**Source:** National Forensic Service (NFS), Jeju Branch
**Report No.:** NFS-JJ-2026-0318-F12
**Date:** March 19, 2026 (expedited processing)

## Evidence Collection

Following review of CCTV footage showing Jeong Minho (Suspect A) entering the villa laundry room at 04:35 AM on March 18, police conducted a forensic examination of the laundry room and its contents.

## Findings

### Washing Machine (Unit 1 of 2)

- Machine had completed a wash cycle. Clothes were inside, still damp.
- **Items recovered:**
  - Dark navy sweater (men's size L) — matches the sweater worn by Jeong Minho in CCTV footage from the evening of March 17
  - Dark jeans (men's size 32)
  - Black socks (1 pair)
  - Grey hand towel

### Luminol Testing

- Luminol spray on the navy sweater revealed **positive fluorescence** on the right sleeve and chest area, consistent with blood contact despite washing.
- Jeans showed faint luminol reaction on the right knee area.

### DNA Analysis

**Note from NFS analyst:** Washing machine cycle significantly degraded biological samples. Results below reflect reduced confidence levels due to sample degradation.

| Sample | Source | Result |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Sweater — right sleeve | Blood residue | **Partial match: Yoon Sejin (87.3% probability)** |
| Sweater — chest area | Blood residue | **Partial match: Yoon Sejin (84.1% probability)** |
| Jeans — right knee | Blood residue | Inconclusive (severely degraded) |
| Hand towel | Mixed biological material | Inconclusive (multiple contributor profiles detected) |

**NFS assessment:** "The DNA profiles recovered from the laundered clothing are consistent with Yoon Sejin but fall below the 99.5% threshold required for positive identification in Korean forensic standards. The partial match strongly suggests Yoon Sejin as the blood source, but cannot be presented as definitive in court. The washing cycle degraded the samples to the point where a defense expert could reasonably challenge the identification."

### Blood Volume Estimate

The luminol pattern and DNA concentration suggest a **small to moderate amount of blood** — consistent with a nosebleed, minor wound, or surface transfer, but **insufficient for a major hemorrhage.** This is consistent with the small blood stain found in Yoon Sejin's bedroom.

## Garbage Bin

The outdoor garbage bin where Jeong Minho deposited a plastic bag at 04:44 AM (per CCTV) had been emptied by municipal collection at 06:00 AM. **Contents not recoverable.**

## Jeong Minho's Explanation (when confronted with forensic results)

> "Sejin had a nosebleed while we were drinking. He gets them when he drinks too much — it's happened before. Taesik saw it too. I helped him with a towel and some of the blood must have gotten on my sweater. That's all. I washed the clothes because they smelled like soju and blood."

## Police Assessment

Jeong Minho's nosebleed explanation is partially corroborated by Oh Taesik (see: alibi_A.md). The DNA evidence is suggestive but not definitive due to sample degradation. **The laundry timing (04:35 AM) and the disposal of a garbage bag (04:44 AM, contents unrecoverable) remain unexplained by the nosebleed account — a nosebleed does not typically require discarding items in an outdoor garbage bin at 4 AM.**
