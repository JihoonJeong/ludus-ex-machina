## Current Analysis for Cliff Base Area

The cliff where Yoon Sejin's phone was recovered faces southwest. Between 03:00 and 05:00 AM on March 18:

- **Strong offshore currents** (2.3-2.8 knots) flowing southwest
- Rising tide combined with offshore currents creates a **dispersal pattern** — any object entering the water in this window would be carried rapidly away from shore
- Water temperature: 13°C (survival time without wetsuit: approximately 1-3 hours)

## Search and Rescue Assessment

The Jeju Coast Guard conducted a 72-hour maritime search beginning March 18, 08:00. No body or additional debris was recovered. The Coast Guard assessment:

> "Given the offshore current conditions during the 03:00-05:00 window, a body entering the water at the cliff base would likely be carried 5-15 km offshore within 6 hours. Recovery becomes extremely unlikely after 24 hours in these conditions."

## Relevance

The tidal data confirms that if Yoon Sejin (or his body) entered the water between 03:00 and 05:00 AM, the strong offshore currents would explain the absence of a body. This timeframe aligns with the phone GPS data showing movement to the cliff at 03:07-03:22.
