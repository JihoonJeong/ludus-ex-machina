# Villa Manager Statement — Kim Jaeho (Suspect E)

**Source:** Witness statement, recorded by Jeju Provincial Police
**Date:** March 18, 2026, 09:00 AM

## Statement on CCTV Shutdown

> "I turned off the CCTV system at about 2 AM. The system had been showing error messages on Camera 4 — the rear garden camera — since yesterday afternoon. Flickering, going black for a few seconds, then coming back. I called the security company during the day but they said they couldn't send someone until Monday.
>
> I decided to do a full system reboot overnight to see if it would fix the issue. The reboot process requires powering off the entire NVR unit, waiting at least two hours for the cache to clear, and then restarting. I've done this before — it's in the manual. I set the restart for 4:30 AM.
>
> I went to sleep after turning it off. I woke up around 5:15 and checked that the system had restarted correctly."

## Verification

- **Camera 4 error logs:** The NVR event log confirms Camera 4 produced 14 error events between 2:00 PM and 11:00 PM on March 17. Error type: "video signal intermittent."
- **Security company records:** Kim Jaeho called Jeju Safe Systems at 3:22 PM on March 17. A service visit was scheduled for March 19 (Monday).
- **NVR manual:** The manual does recommend a "full power cycle with 2+ hour cache clear" for persistent signal errors.
- **However:** The NVR has an option to reboot only the affected camera channel without shutting down the entire system. Kim Jaeho stated he was not aware of this option.

## Background — Kim Jaeho

- Age: 58
- Occupation: Villa caretaker (3 years at Villa Haemoru)
- Former profession: Fisherman (retired 2020)
- No criminal record
- Known to local community; described as reliable
- Monthly salary: ₩2,800,000

## Connection to Yoon Sejin

Kim Jaeho stated he had no prior relationship with any of the crew members before their arrival on March 15. Villa Haemoru is a rental property booked through a production company account.

## Police Assessment

The CCTV shutdown has a documented technical justification. However, the timing is notable — the system was off during the exact window of the disappearance. The availability of a channel-specific reboot option that Kim Jaeho did not use raises a question about whether the full shutdown was necessary. No evidence of coordination between Kim Jaeho and any suspect has been found.
