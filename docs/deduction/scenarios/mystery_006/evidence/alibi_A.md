# Alibi Statement — Jeong Minho (Suspect A)

**Source:** Witness statement by Oh Taesik (Suspect C), recorded by Jeju Provincial Police
**Date:** March 18, 2026, 10:30 AM

## Oh Taesik's Statement

> "After the director [Yoon Sejin] went to his room around midnight, Minho [Jeong Minho] and I kept drinking in the living room. We were talking about the shoot schedule and some personal stuff. I remember Minho went out briefly to buy more drinks — he came back with soju from the convenience store down the road.
>
> We kept drinking until maybe around 2 AM. I was getting pretty drunk by then. I remember Minho said something like 'let's call it a night' and I went to my room. I think Minho stayed in the living room for a bit to clean up the bottles, but I'm not sure — I went straight to bed.
>
> I didn't hear anything unusual during the night. I slept until about 6:30."

## Follow-up Question: Nosebleed Incident

**Q: Did Yoon Sejin have a nosebleed during the evening?**

> "Oh, yeah — that's right. Before Sejin went to his room, maybe around 11:30 or so, he had a nosebleed. Not a bad one, but enough to drip on his shirt. Minho grabbed a towel from the kitchen and helped him. Sejin held the towel to his nose for a minute, said 'this always happens when I drink too much,' and then went to clean up in the bathroom. He came back and we drank a bit more before he went to bed.
>
> Actually, some of the blood might have gotten on Minho's sweater too — Minho was sitting right next to him when it started. I remember Minho saying something like 'ah, it got on my clothes' but he didn't seem bothered."

## Jeong Minho's Own Statement (summary)

Jeong Minho's account is consistent with Oh Taesik's:

> "Taesik and I were drinking in the living room. Sejin had a nosebleed before he went to bed — it happens to him sometimes when he drinks. I helped him with a towel and got some blood on my sleeve. I went to get more soju around 1:40. We finished around 2, maybe a bit after. I cleaned up and went to bed. I woke up around 4:30 — couldn't sleep well, jet lag from the Busan trip earlier that week. I decided to wash my clothes since they smelled like soju and had blood on them. Then I went back to bed."

## Police Assessment

Oh Taesik's statement provides an alibi for Jeong Minho until approximately 2:00 AM. The nosebleed account is corroborated by Oh Taesik as an independent witness, which lends credibility to Jeong Minho's explanation for the blood on his clothing.

**However:**
1. Oh Taesik acknowledges he was intoxicated and went directly to bed — **he cannot account for Jeong Minho's activities after approximately 2:00 AM.**
2. The nosebleed reportedly occurred around 23:30, but Jeong Minho did not wash the stained clothes until **04:35 AM — over 5 hours later.** The delay is inconsistent with simply wanting to clean a stain.
3. CCTV shows Jeong Minho disposing of a garbage bag at 04:44 AM. **A nosebleed cleanup does not explain what was in the discarded bag.**
