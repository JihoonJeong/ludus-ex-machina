# Case Brief: The Jeju Disappearance

## Incident Summary

On the morning of March 18, 2026, documentary director **Yoon Sejin** (42) was reported missing from a private villa in Jungmun, Jeju Island. Yoon had been staying at the villa with four members of his production crew for a 4-day filming schedule.

Yoon was last confirmed present at approximately 1:00 AM. When the crew gathered for a 7:00 AM call time, Yoon's bedroom was empty. A small amount of blood was found on the bedroom floor. His mobile phone was later recovered at the base of a coastal cliff approximately 800 meters from the villa. No body has been found.

## Initial Assessment

Jeju Provincial Police have classified this as a suspicious disappearance. Suicide, accident, and foul play are all under consideration. The investigative division has been deployed.

## Suspects

Five individuals present at the villa have been identified as persons of interest:

- **Suspect A (Mr. Jeong Minho):** Producer and co-CEO of the production company.

- **Suspect B (Ms. Han Soyul):** Director of Photography on the documentary.

- **Suspect C (Mr. Oh Taesik):** Sound Director on the documentary.

- **Suspect D (Ms. Bae Yuna):** Assistant Director on the documentary.

- **Suspect E (Mr. Kim Jaeho):** Caretaker of the villa.
