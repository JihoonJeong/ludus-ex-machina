## Seo Jinwoo's Explanation

**Q: Why do you have a key to the gallery's service corridor?**
> "Chaeeun gave me a key months ago when I was doing installation setup. I worked late nights and she didn't want to give me a security card for temporary access. I forgot to return it."

**Q: Why do you have a floor plan with the sprinkler valve marked?**
> "I needed to know the gallery layout for my installation — where the water pipes run, where the electrical outlets are. It's standard for installation artists. The sprinkler location was relevant because I needed to know what areas had water protection for my mixed-media pieces."

**Q: Why is there turpentine in your studio?**
> "I'm a mixed-media artist. Turpentine is a basic studio supply. Every artist has it."

## Police Assessment

The studio search reveals:
1. **The stolen artworks** are in Seo Jinwoo's possession (he claims he removed them on March 18, before the fire)
2. **Turpentine** — the same accelerant used in the arson — is present in his studio
3. **A key to the service corridor** — the unmonitored entry point to the gallery — was in his desk
4. **An annotated floor plan** with the sprinkler valve location circled

Each item has an innocent explanation. Collectively, they establish that Seo Jinwoo had: the accelerant, the means of entry (service corridor key), and knowledge of how to disable the fire suppression system.

**However, his Seoul alibi (see: seoul_alibi_B.md) places him 325 km away less than 5 hours before the fire.** The physical evidence from the studio is significant but cannot be evaluated without resolving the alibi question.
