# Plagiarism Dispute — Noh Youngtaek (Suspect C) vs Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B)

**Source:** Korean Artists Association mediation records + social media posts
**Obtained by:** Busan Metropolitan Police

## Background

Noh Youngtaek (Suspect C, painter) publicly accused Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B, installation artist) of plagiarizing compositional elements from his 2024 painting series "Fractured Horizons" in Seo's "Dissolution Series."

## Timeline

- **November 2025:** Noh posts on Instagram comparing his "Fractured Horizons #3" (2024) with Seo's "Dissolution Series #7" (2025), captioning: "When 'inspiration' crosses the line. @seojinwoo_art explain this."
- **December 2025:** Korean Artists Association receives formal complaint from Noh. Mediation scheduled for April 2026.
- **January 2026:** Seo responds publicly: "Noh's work and mine share a common aesthetic tradition. Claiming ownership of abstract geometric patterns is absurd."
- **February 2026:** Noh's attorney sends cease-and-desist to Seo regarding exhibition of "Dissolution Series" pieces.

## Relevance to the Fire

Noh Youngtaek had expressed a desire to prevent the exhibition from proceeding with Seo Jinwoo's works. In a message to curator Han Mijeong (January 28, 2026):

> "If Seo's plagiarized works are shown alongside mine, it legitimizes his theft. I'd rather the entire exhibition be cancelled than share a wall with a plagiarist."

## Noh Youngtaek's Alibi

Noh Youngtaek states he was at his home in Busan (Suyeong-gu) on the night of March 19-20. His wife confirms he was home by 10 PM and did not leave. No independent verification available (no CCTV at residential apartment).

## Police Assessment

Noh Youngtaek had a motive to disrupt the exhibition but his preferred outcome was exhibition cancellation, not gallery destruction. Arson would destroy his own exhibited paintings as well. His alibi is unverified but spousal.
