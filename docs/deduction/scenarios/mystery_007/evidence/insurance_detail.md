# Fire Insurance Policy — Art Space Pado

**Source:** Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance, policy disclosure
**Policy No.:** SF-2025-0915-BG447

## Policy Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Policyholder | Im Chaeeun (Suspect A), as owner of Art Space Pado |
| Coverage type | Commercial fire insurance + fine art rider |
| **Total coverage** | **₩800,000,000** |
| Building/interior | ₩300M |
| **Artwork coverage** | **₩500M** (based on submitted inventory) |
| **Effective date** | **September 15, 2025** (6 months before fire) |
| Previous policy | None — gallery was previously uninsured |
| Premium | ₩4,200,000/year |
| Payment | Paid in full (annual lump sum) |

## Underwriting Notes

- Im Chaeeun applied for the policy on September 10, 2025, at Samsung Fire Busan branch.
- The art inventory submitted for the fine art rider listed 35 works with a total declared value of ₩500M.
- The insurance agent noted: "Ms. Im stated she was insuring the gallery for the first time due to an upcoming series of high-value exhibitions. She mentioned that several works valued over ₩100M each would be displayed."
- **No independent appraisal was conducted** at the time of policy issuance. Samsung Fire relied on the inventory values provided by the policyholder.

## Claim Potential

If the fire is confirmed as arson by an unknown party (i.e., not the policyholder), Im Chaeeun could claim up to ₩800M. Given that the gallery's total actual asset value (building + artworks) is estimated at ₩400-500M by independent assessors, **the insurance coverage may exceed the actual value of the gallery's contents by ₩300-400M.**
