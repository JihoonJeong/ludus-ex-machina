# Artist Complaint — Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B)

**Source:** Email records, Im Chaeeun's gallery email account
**Obtained by:** Busan Metropolitan Police, court order

## Email Thread: Exhibition Placement Dispute

**Seo Jinwoo → Im Chaeeun, March 12, 2026, 10:15 AM:**
> "Chaeeun, I visited the gallery yesterday to check the installation layout. My three pieces are in the back corner behind the partition wall. Nobody walking in will even see them. Youngtaek's paintings are on the main wall — front and center. This is a group show, not a solo exhibition for Noh Youngtaek.
>
> I've spent six months on the Dissolution Series. The placement is insulting."

**Im Chaeeun → Seo Jinwoo, March 12, 2026, 14:30:**
> "Jinwoo, the layout was designed by Mijeong [Han Mijeong, curator]. Installation pieces work better in an enclosed space — that's standard curation. The corner creates an immersive environment for your work. I think you'll see it differently at the opening."

**Seo Jinwoo → Im Chaeeun, March 14, 2026, 09:45:**
> "I spoke with Mijeong. She said YOU decided the layout, not her. Don't hide behind the curator. If my pieces aren't moved to the main hall by March 17, I'm pulling them."

**Im Chaeeun → Seo Jinwoo, March 14, 2026, 11:20:**
> "The layout is final. Moving your installation would require restructuring the entire exhibition. I'm sorry you're unhappy, but this is my gallery and my decision."

**Seo Jinwoo → Im Chaeeun, March 17, 2026, 15:30:**
> "I'm withdrawing my three pieces. The placement is unacceptable."

**Im Chaeeun → Seo Jinwoo, March 17, 2026, 16:45:**
> "Jinwoo, please reconsider. Let's discuss the placement."

*No further emails in this thread.*

## Police Assessment

The email chain documents escalating conflict between Seo Jinwoo and the gallery owner over exhibition placement. Seo Jinwoo's stated reason for removing his works (displeasure with placement) is supported by the email record. However, the dispute also establishes that Seo Jinwoo harbored strong resentment toward the gallery and its owner in the days immediately preceding the fire.
