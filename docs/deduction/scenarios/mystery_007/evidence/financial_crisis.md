## Renovation Plans

Police found a folder on Im Chaeeun's office computer titled "Art Space Pado 2.0 — Renovation Plan":
- Architectural renderings dated January 2026 from a Busan design firm
- Renovation budget estimate: ₩350M
- Concept: "Transform gallery into mixed-use creative space with café and co-working"
- **Funding source noted in the document: "Insurance settlement + investor round"**

## Im Chaeeun's Statement

> "The gallery has been struggling, yes. But the renovation plan is aspirational — I was exploring options, not planning to burn down my own gallery. The insurance was purchased because we were starting to show higher-value works. Any responsible gallery owner would get insurance."

## Police Assessment

Im Chaeeun has severe financial motive:
1. ₩275M in outstanding debts
2. Gallery losing ₩7-10M/month
3. Renovation plan explicitly references "insurance settlement" as a funding source
4. Over-insurance: ₩800M coverage on ₩400-500M actual value

**This is the strongest financial motive among all suspects.**
