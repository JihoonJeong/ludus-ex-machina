# Missing Artworks Report

**Source:** Busan Metropolitan Police, Art Crimes Unit
**Date:** March 21, 2026

## Inventory

The gallery's pre-fire exhibition inventory (confirmed by curator Han Mijeong) listed 23 artworks from 5 artists. Post-fire inspection found 3 artworks missing — only empty frames remained.

### Missing Works

| # | Title | Artist | Medium | Estimated Value |
|---|-------|--------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | "Dissolution Series #7" | **Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B)** | Mixed media installation | ₩80M |
| 2 | "Dissolution Series #12" | **Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B)** | Mixed media installation | ₩120M |
| 3 | "Dissolution Series #15" | **Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B)** | Mixed media installation | ₩95M |

**All three missing works belong to Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B).** Combined estimated value: ₩295M.

## Frame Analysis

- Frames were gallery-standard aluminum mounting frames
- Paintings/installations were removed by unscrewing the rear mounting brackets — **a careful, deliberate removal requiring tools (screwdriver) and approximately 5-10 minutes per work**
- No damage to frames; removal was not hurried
- Total estimated removal time: 15-30 minutes for all three works

## Seo Jinwoo's Statement

When informed that the missing works were his:

> "I removed those pieces myself on March 18 — two days before the fire. I told Chaeeun [Im Chaeeun, gallery owner] I was pulling them from the exhibition. We had a disagreement about how they were displayed. She put my installation pieces in a corner where nobody would see them — it felt disrespectful. I took them back to my studio.
>
> You can verify — I sent her an email about it. I had every right to withdraw my works before the exhibition opened. The show hadn't started yet."

## Verification

- **Email confirmation:** Seo Jinwoo sent an email to Im Chaeeun on March 17, 2026, at 15:30, stating: "I'm withdrawing my three pieces. The placement is unacceptable." Im Chaeeun replied at 16:45: "Jinwoo, please reconsider. Let's discuss the placement."
- **No further email exchange** on this topic was found.
- **Gallery visitor log:** No entry recorded for Seo Jinwoo on March 18. However, the service corridor (no card reader) provides unmonitored access.

## Police Assessment

Seo Jinwoo's explanation is partially supported by the email exchange showing his displeasure with the placement. However:
1. The gallery has no record of his entry on March 18 to remove the works
2. The works were later found at his studio (see: `studio_search.md`)
3. **If the works were removed on March 18 as claimed, why were the empty frames left on the wall for two days without the gallery owner reporting the removal to police or the other artists?**

Im Chaeeun's statement on this: "I assumed he'd bring them back after we talked. I didn't want to escalate the situation before the opening."
