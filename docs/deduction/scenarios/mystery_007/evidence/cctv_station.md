# Busan Station CCTV — Suspect Identification Attempt

**Source:** KORAIL Busan Station security footage
**Analyzed by:** Busan Metropolitan Police CCTV Analysis Unit
**Date range:** March 20, 2026, 00:30 – 02:00

## Review Summary

Police reviewed Busan Station CCTV footage for the arrival window of the last Seoul→Busan KTX trains on March 19-20.

## Relevant Footage

### KTX-143 Arrival (01:12 AM)

Approximately 85 passengers disembarked from KTX-143 at Platform 3.

At **01:15 AM**, a male figure was captured on the east exit camera:
- **Build:** Medium height (~175cm), slim build
- **Clothing:** Black hooded sweatshirt (hood up), dark pants, black backpack
- **Face:** Mostly obscured by hood and **black disposable mask**
- **Behavior:** Walked directly toward the taxi stand without stopping. Did not use the station's convenience store or restroom.

### Comparison with Seo Jinwoo

| Feature | CCTV Figure | Seo Jinwoo |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Height | ~175cm | 174cm |
| Build | Slim | Slim |
| Hair | Not visible (hood) | Black, medium length |
| Gait | Normal pace, slight forward lean | — |
| Distinguishing marks | None visible | Small tattoo on left wrist (not visible in footage) |

### Taxi Stand

The figure entered a taxi at approximately 01:17 AM. The taxi license plate was partially captured: **부산 32바 7X XX** (last two digits unclear due to camera angle).

Police contacted the Busan Taxi Cooperative. Due to the partial plate and the late hour (fewer dispatch records), the specific taxi has **not been identified.** The Haeundae-gu area (location of Art Space Pado) is approximately 30 minutes from Busan Station by taxi.

### Other Passengers

No other disembarking passengers from KTX-143 exhibited unusual behavior or matched any suspect profile.

## Police Assessment

The CCTV footage shows an **unidentified masked individual** arriving at Busan Station at 01:15 AM on the last KTX from Seoul. The individual's build is consistent with Seo Jinwoo but **positive identification is not possible** due to the mask, hood, and limited camera angle.

**If this individual is Seo Jinwoo:**
- Departed Seoul (Anguk-dong area) after 22:08
- Caught KTX-143 at Seoul Station (22:30 departure)
- Arrived Busan Station at 01:12
- Taxi to Haeundae (Art Space Pado): ~30 min → arrival ~01:45
- Fire at 03:00 → approximately 75 minutes available at the gallery

**This timeline is physically feasible but unconfirmed.**
