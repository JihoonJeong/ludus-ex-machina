# Case Brief: The Burning Gallery

## Incident Summary

At approximately 3:00 AM on March 20, 2026, a fire broke out at **Art Space Pado**, an independent gallery in Haeundae-gu, Busan. The fire destroyed approximately 80% of the artworks prepared for a five-artist group exhibition scheduled to open on March 23.

Fire investigators determined the blaze was **intentionally set**. Additionally, three artworks were found to have been removed from the gallery prior to the fire — only empty frames remained on the walls.

The estimated total damage is ₩600 million. The combined incident of arson and art theft has been referred to the Busan Metropolitan Police Serious Crimes Division.

## Initial Assessment

Police are investigating the fire as intentional arson combined with art theft. Whether the arson and the theft were committed by the same person, or represent separate criminal acts, is under investigation.

## Suspects

Five individuals with access to the gallery have been identified as persons of interest:

- **Suspect A (Ms. Im Chaeeun):** Owner of Art Space Pado.

- **Suspect B (Mr. Seo Jinwoo):** Participating artist (installation art) in the upcoming exhibition.

- **Suspect C (Mr. Noh Youngtaek):** Participating artist (painting) in the upcoming exhibition.

- **Suspect D (Ms. Han Mijeong):** Curator of the exhibition.

- **Suspect E (Mr. Joo Wonseok):** Security guard and building caretaker.
