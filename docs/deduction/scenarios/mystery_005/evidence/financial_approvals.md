## CFO Statement (Choi Seoyeon)

When questioned about the approvals, CFO Choi Seoyeon stated:

> "The CTO submitted these as technology infrastructure expenses. The invoices included technical specifications I'm not qualified to evaluate. Our process is that the CTO certifies the technical necessity, and I approve the financials. The amounts were within the CTO's departmental budget authority after the Series B funding. I had no reason to question them."

## Notes

- All three approvals were made during normal business hours (9-10 AM), consistent with Ms. Choi's typical work pattern.
- The electronic signatures used Ms. Choi's registered certificate, accessed from her office workstation.
- No evidence that Ms. Choi's electronic signature was forged or used without her knowledge.
- PayBridge's payment policy does not require the CFO to independently verify vendor legitimacy — only to confirm budget availability and proper documentation.
