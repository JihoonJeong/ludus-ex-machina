# Internal Audit Report — PayBridge Inc.

**Prepared by:** Sejong Accounting (Mr. Kang Dohyun, Lead Partner)
**Date:** March 3, 2026
**Classification:** Confidential

## Summary of Findings

During the routine Q4 2025 financial review, the following anomalies were identified:

### Unauthorized Transfers

Three corporate entities received a total of ₩1.2 billion from PayBridge operating accounts between December 2025 and February 2026:

| Recipient | Amount | Date | Invoice Description |
|-----------|--------|------|-------------------|
| Bluewave Solutions Ltd. | ₩380M | Dec 18, 2025 | "Cloud infrastructure upgrade" |
| Nextform Digital Inc. | ₩450M | Jan 22, 2026 | "AI model licensing fee" |
| Greenfield Data Corp. | ₩370M | Feb 14, 2026 | "Data compliance consulting" |

### Shell Company Assessment

All three entities were registered between October and November 2025. Corporate registry checks reveal:

- **Bluewave Solutions Ltd.:** Registered Oct 12, 2025. Address: Virtual office in Pangyo. No employees, no website, no tax filings.
- **Nextform Digital Inc.:** Registered Oct 28, 2025. Address: Virtual office in Gangnam. No employees, no website, no tax filings.
- **Greenfield Data Corp.:** Registered Nov 5, 2025. Address: Virtual office in Yeouido. No employees, no website, no tax filings.

All three entities share the same registered agent: a legal services firm specializing in rapid corporate registration. The registered agent has confirmed the registrations were made online with minimal identity verification.

### Fund Tracing

Funds transferred to the three entities were subsequently moved through two intermediary accounts before reaching a final account at Shinhan Bank. The final account holder information has been requested from the bank but has not yet been provided to Sejong Accounting.

### Conclusion

The transfers bear hallmarks of a systematic embezzlement scheme using shell companies. The invoices reference services that PayBridge has no record of contracting. The internal authorization trail is under review.
