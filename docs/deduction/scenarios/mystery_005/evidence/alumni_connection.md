# Alumni Connection — Lee Haneul and Kim Junhyuk

**Source:** Seoul Metropolitan Police, background investigation

## Finding

During background checks on the shell company registrations, police identified that the individual who served as the contact person for all three shell company registrations — **Kim Junhyuk** — is a university classmate of Suspect C, **Lee Haneul (Marketing Director)**.

## Relationship Details

- Both attended Yonsei University, Department of Business Administration, Class of 2014.
- University alumni directory confirms overlapping enrollment (2010-2014).
- Social media analysis: 3 mutual connections on LinkedIn, 12 mutual friends on Facebook.
- Last confirmed public interaction: Alumni reunion event, June 2024 (group photo posted on Facebook).

## Kim Junhyuk Profile

- Age: 34
- Occupation: Freelance business consultant
- Registered address: Seocho-gu, Seoul
- Prior record: One civil judgment (contract dispute, 2022, settled)
- Served as the registered contact for Bluewave Solutions, Nextform Digital, and Greenfield Data Corp.
- When questioned by police, Kim stated he was hired online through a freelance platform to serve as a corporate formation agent. He claims he never met the person who hired him in person and was paid ₩1.5M per registration via bank transfer.

## Lee Haneul's Statement

When asked about her relationship with Kim Junhyuk:

> "We were in the same graduating class but I wouldn't call us close. We have mutual friends from university. I haven't spoken to him individually in over two years. I had no idea he was involved in any of this."

## Police Assessment

The alumni connection is noted but no direct evidence links Lee Haneul to Kim Junhyuk's activities related to the shell companies. The bank transfer payments to Kim originated from an account not linked to any of the four suspects (traced to a prepaid account opened with minimal ID verification).
