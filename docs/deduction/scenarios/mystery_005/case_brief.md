# Case Brief: The Vanishing Fund

## Incident Summary

Between December 2025 and February 2026, approximately ₩1.2 billion was transferred from the operating accounts of **PayBridge Inc.**, a fintech startup based in Seongsu-dong, Seoul, to three previously unregistered corporate entities. The transfers were discovered during a routine internal audit in early March 2026.

PayBridge had recently closed a Series B funding round of ₩3.8 billion. The company's board of directors has referred the matter to the Seoul Metropolitan Police Financial Crimes Unit.

## Initial Assessment

Police have opened a formal investigation into the unauthorized fund transfers. The method of access to financial systems and the identity of the person responsible are under investigation.

## Suspects

Four individuals have been identified as persons of interest:

- **Suspect A (Ms. Choi Seoyeon):** Chief Financial Officer at PayBridge Inc.

- **Suspect B (Mr. Park Jaemin):** Chief Technology Officer at PayBridge Inc.

- **Suspect C (Ms. Lee Haneul):** Marketing Director at PayBridge Inc.

- **Suspect D (Mr. Kang Dohyun):** Partner at Sejong Accounting, PayBridge's external audit firm.
