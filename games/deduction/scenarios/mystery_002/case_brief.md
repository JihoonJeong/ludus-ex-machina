# Case Brief: The Silent Partner

On the evening of March 15, 2026, Mr. Choi Daeho (58), CEO of Hanjin Technologies, was found dead in his office at the company headquarters in Pangyo. The building security guard discovered the body at approximately 10:30 PM during a routine check.

## Initial Assessment

The cause and circumstances of death are under investigation. The major crimes unit has been assigned to the case.

## Suspects

Three individuals have been identified as persons of interest:

- **Suspect A (Ms. Kang Soyeon):** VP of Operations at Hanjin Technologies. Has been with the company for 12 years.

- **Suspect B (Mr. Yoon Jaemin):** Co-founder of Hanjin Technologies and minority shareholder.

- **Suspect C (Mr. Han Dongwoo):** Personal assistant to the CEO for the past 4 years.
