# Separate Consulting Arrangement — Kang Dohyun (Sejong Accounting)

**Source:** PayBridge Inc. contract records + Sejong Accounting disclosure

## Finding

In addition to the standard audit engagement (₩60M/year), PayBridge Inc. has a separate consulting contract with **Kang Dohyun personally** (not through Sejong Accounting):

### Contract Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Title | "Strategic Financial Advisory Services" |
| Parties | PayBridge Inc. ↔ Kang Dohyun (individual capacity) |
| Signed | September 15, 2025 |
| Fee | ₩5M/month + performance bonus |
| Scope | "Tax optimization, M&A advisory, investor relations support" |
| Authorized by | CEO Kim Taewon |
| Duration | 12 months |

### Potential Conflict of Interest

Kang Dohyun simultaneously serves as:
1. **External auditor** (through Sejong Accounting) — responsible for verifying PayBridge's financial statements
2. **Personal consultant** (individual contract) — advising on financial strategy

This arrangement potentially violates auditor independence requirements under the External Audit Act (외부감사법).

### Kang Dohyun's Statement

> "The consulting arrangement is entirely separate from the audit engagement. I recused myself from directly reviewing any transactions I advised on. The CEO approached me because of my familiarity with the company's financials. There is no conflict of interest."

### Sejong Accounting's Position

Sejong Accounting was not aware of the personal consulting contract until the police investigation. The firm has initiated an internal review.

### Police Assessment

- The consulting fees (₩60M audit + ₩60M consulting = ₩120M/year) represent a significant financial interest in maintaining the PayBridge relationship.
- However, no evidence directly links the consulting arrangement to the shell company transfers.
- The audit report that discovered the embezzlement was prepared by Kang Dohyun's team — if he were involved in the scheme, it would be unusual for his own team to flag the anomalies.
- **Assessment: Conflict of interest present, but motive is to maintain the consulting income, not to embezzle. His team discovering the fraud actually argues against his involvement.**
