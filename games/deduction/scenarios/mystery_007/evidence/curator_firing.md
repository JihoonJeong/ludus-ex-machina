**Source:** Art Space Pado employment records
**Obtained by:** Busan Metropolitan Police

## Termination Email

> "Mijeong,
>
> After careful consideration, I've decided not to renew your contract after the current exhibition. Your last working day will be March 31, 2026.
>

## Police Assessment

Han Mijeong was terminated one week before the fire. Her response email ("Don't expect me to make this easy") could be interpreted as a threat, though it could also refer to professional non-cooperation. She retained active security card access to the gallery at the time of the fire. However, her security card was not used during the relevant timeframe (see: security_card_log.md).
