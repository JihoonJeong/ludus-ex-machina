## Verification

- **Building perimeter walking time:** Police walked the full exterior perimeter of the building (including back alley, parking structure entrance, and loading dock). **Measured time: 8-9 minutes at normal walking pace.** Consistent with a 10-minute gap (allowing for brief pauses to check doors/windows).
- **Previous duty logs:** Review of 30 days of Joo Wonseok's logs shows a consistent pattern of perimeter rounds at irregular intervals (every 1-3 hours). The 02:30 round is within normal pattern.
- **No CCTV coverage** of the back alley or service corridor area during the relevant timeframe (gallery's own cameras are interior-only; neighboring building cameras do not cover this angle).

## Police Assessment

Joo Wonseok's 02:40 entry is consistent with his stated perimeter patrol. The 10-minute duration matches the measured walking time. His duty log pattern over the preceding month supports the regularity of these rounds.

**However:**
1. The duty log is self-reported (handwritten by Joo Wonseok himself) — no independent verification of actual patrol activities
2. The 02:40 entry places him at the front door **35 minutes before the fire** — he was the last person to use the security system before the fire alarm
3. No camera coverage of the service corridor or back alley means **any person approaching from the parking structure side would not be recorded**
4. The service corridor (key access only, no card reader) remains an unmonitored entry point

**Joo Wonseok's card entry at 02:40 does not implicate him — it may simply record his return from patrol. But it also cannot exclude the possibility that someone else entered the building via the service corridor during or after his patrol, undetected.**
