# Alibi Statement — Seo Jinwoo (Suspect B)

**Source:** Witness statements + social media + venue records
**Compiled by:** Busan Metropolitan Police

## Seo Jinwoo's Account

> "On the evening of March 19, I was in Seoul for the opening reception of a group exhibition at Gallery Hyundai in Samcheong-dong. The event ran from 7 PM to about 10 PM. Afterward, I went to a bar in Anguk-dong with some artist friends. I stayed until late — maybe midnight — and then crashed at my friend Jiyeon's apartment in Hannam-dong. I took the first KTX back to Busan the next morning."

## Verification

### Gallery Hyundai Event

- **Event:** "New Horizons: Contemporary Installation" opening reception
- **Date:** March 19, 2026, 19:00 – 22:00
- **Venue:** Gallery Hyundai, 14 Samcheong-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul
- **Guest list:** Gallery Hyundai confirmed Seo Jinwoo checked in at 19:15.

### Social Media Evidence

- **Instagram post by Seo Jinwoo (@seojinwoo_art):**
  - Photo with two other artists in front of a large installation piece
  - Posted: March 19, 2026, 19:23
  - Location tag: Gallery Hyundai, Seoul
  - Geotagged metadata confirmed as authentic

- **Instagram story by @artist_parkjiyeon (Park Jiyeon, fellow artist):**
  - Video of Seo Jinwoo speaking with a gallery director
  - Posted: March 19, 2026, 20:45
  - Location tag: Gallery Hyundai

### Post-Event: Bar Moon, Anguk-dong

- **Credit card receipt:** ₩45,000, card registered to Seo Jinwoo, at "Bar Moon" in Anguk-dong. **Transaction time: 22:08.**
- **Bar staff statement:** "A group of about five or six people came in around 10 PM. I can't say exactly when each person left. We were busy that night."

### Witness Statements on Bar Duration

- **Park Jiyeon (artist):** "We went to Bar Moon after the event. Jinwoo was there... I think I left around 11 PM. I was pretty drunk. I think Jinwoo was still there when I left? Actually, I'm not sure. It's a bit hazy."
- **Lee Dongwook (gallery director):** "I went to Bar Moon briefly but left around 10:30. Seo Jinwoo was there at that point."

### Overnight Stay Claim

Seo Jinwoo claims he stayed at Park Jiyeon's apartment in Hannam-dong.

**Park Jiyeon's response:**
> "What? No, Jinwoo didn't sleep at my place. I went home alone around 11 and went to bed. I haven't hosted anyone at my apartment in months. Maybe he's confusing it with a different night? Or maybe he said Hannam-dong and meant somewhere else? I don't know."

## Police Assessment

Seo Jinwoo has a **verified alibi in Seoul until 22:08** (credit card receipt at Bar Moon). After 22:08, his whereabouts are unverified:
- Witness accounts of his presence at the bar after 22:08 are vague and alcohol-impaired
- His claimed overnight stay at Park Jiyeon's apartment is **directly contradicted** by Park Jiyeon

**Seoul and Busan are approximately 325 km apart.** The question of whether Seo Jinwoo could have traveled from Seoul to Busan between 22:08 and 03:00 (approximately 5 hours) is a matter of transportation feasibility. See: `ktx_schedule.md` for relevant train schedules.
