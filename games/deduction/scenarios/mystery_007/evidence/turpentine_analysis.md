### Manufacturer Identification

The chemical profile matches **Shinhan Art Materials "Professional Grade Turpentine"** (product code: SH-TRP-5000).

- Manufactured at: Shinhan Gimpo factory
- **Production lot: L2025-0814** (identified via trace additive ratios unique to each production batch)
- Production date: August 14, 2025
- Distribution: Sold through art supply retailers in Seoul, Busan, and Daegu

### Distribution Records

Shinhan Art Materials provided sales records for lot L2025-0814:
- Total production: 200 units (5L containers)
- Distributed to 12 retailers nationwide
- **Busan retailers that received this lot:** Hwamok Art Supply (Seomyeon), Art World (Haeundae)
- Individual customer purchases are not tracked by Shinhan (retailers handle point-of-sale)

## NFS Conclusion

> "The turpentine samples recovered from the fire scene (FS-01, FS-02) and the sample from Seo Jinwoo's studio (SS-01) are **consistent in chemical composition and production lot.** All three samples originate from the same production batch (Shinhan lot L2025-0814).
>
> This finding establishes that the accelerant used in the arson came from the **same production lot** as the turpentine in Seo Jinwoo's studio. However, approximately 200 containers from this lot were distributed to multiple retailers. **The match narrows the source but does not uniquely identify the studio sample as the fire accelerant's origin.**"

## Police Assessment

The turpentine lot match is significant circumstantial evidence linking the fire accelerant to Seo Jinwoo's supplies. Combined with the studio search findings (turpentine container, service corridor key, annotated floor plan), this strengthens the physical evidence case against Suspect B.

However, the NFS caution is noted: the same lot was sold through multiple retailers, meaning other purchasers could theoretically possess the same product. **The strength of this evidence depends on whether Suspect B's alibi can be broken.**
