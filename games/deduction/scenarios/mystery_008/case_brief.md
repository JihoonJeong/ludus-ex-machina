# Case Brief — The Silent Lab
**Case Number:** UC-CID-2026-0402
**Assigned Unit:** University Campus Investigation Division
**Date of Incident:** April 2, 2026
**Location:** Hanyang University, Department of Chemistry, Laboratory 307 (3rd Floor, Science Building 3)

---

## Incident Summary

At 6:20 AM on April 2, 2026, a lab technician arriving for the morning shift discovered graduate student **Yoon Jaehee** (age 27, 4th-year doctoral candidate, Department of Chemistry) unresponsive at a workbench in Laboratory 307. Emergency services were dispatched immediately. Yoon Jaehee was pronounced dead at the scene at 6:47 AM.

Preliminary assessment indicated the death was suspicious. The Major Crimes Investigation Unit has been assigned. Laboratory 307 has been sealed and is currently being processed as a crime scene.

---

## Suspects Under Investigation

- **Dr. Kim Junghyun** — Lab Director, Professor
- **Park Minsu** — Postdoc Researcher
- **Lee Soyeon** — Lab Technician
- **Prof. Cho Youngwoo** — Visiting Professor

All four individuals had authorized access to Laboratory 307 and were associated with the deceased's academic environment. All have been interviewed and are cooperating with the investigation.

---

## Status

Investigation is currently underway. Forensic analysis of the laboratory and victim is ongoing. Further interviews are scheduled.
