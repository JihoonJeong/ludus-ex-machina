# Academic Integrity Committee — Case Notice
**Hanyang University, Office of Academic Integrity**
**Case Reference:** AIC-2026-031
**Notice Date:** March 25, 2026
**Classification:** Confidential — Restricted to Parties and Reviewers

---

## Case Information

**Nature of Allegation:** Unauthorized use of unpublished research data in doctoral thesis
**Complainant:** Park Minsu (Postdoctoral Researcher, Chemistry Department, Kim Lab)
**Respondent:** Yoon Jaehee (Doctoral Candidate, 4th year, Chemistry Department, Kim Lab)
**Filed:** March 21, 2026

---

## Summary of Complaint

Park Minsu alleges that Yoon Jaehee incorporated data and analytical methodology from his unpublished PM-series synthesis research into Chapter 4, Section 4.3 of her doctoral thesis draft without attribution, permission, or acknowledgment. He further alleges that this research represents approximately 24 months of independent work that has not yet been submitted for external publication.

---

## Committee Actions

Following preliminary review, the Committee has determined that the complaint meets the threshold for formal investigation.

**External Reviewer Assigned:** Prof. Cho Youngwoo, Department of Organic Chemistry, Seoul National University (currently serving as Visiting Professor, Hanyang University)

Prof. Cho has been selected as independent reviewer on the basis of his expertise in the relevant area of synthetic chemistry. His assignment is procedural and does not imply any presumption of findings.

**Review Timeline:** External review report expected by April 15, 2026.

---

## Notice to Parties

Both the complainant (Park Minsu) and the respondent (Yoon Jaehee) have been notified of this case reference by sealed letter dated March 25, 2026. Yoon Jaehee signed delivery confirmation on March 26, 2026.

Pending review, Yoon Jaehee's thesis submission timeline is formally suspended.
