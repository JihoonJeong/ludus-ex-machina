## Audit Scope

Full inventory of all chemicals present in Lab 307. All containers cross-referenced against the Lab 307 Chemical Inventory Register (last updated March 15, 2026, by Lee Soyeon).

## Critical Finding: Mislabeled Container at Bench 3

**Container description:** 500 mL amber glass bottle
**Affixed label:** "NaCl — Sodium Chloride, 99.9% purity, 500g, Lot #KC-2024-0317, Daejung Chemicals"
**Location:** Bench 3 open reagent shelf, front position

**Field test result (azide ion strip test, performed 09:47):** STRONGLY POSITIVE

**Confirmatory ICP-MS analysis (submitted April 2, results April 3):** Container contents identified as **Sodium Azide (NaN₃)**, purity approximately 98%. Total mass: approximately 310g.

**Conclusion:** The container labeled as sodium chloride contains sodium azide.

---

## Cross-Reference with Inventory Register

- **Inventory Register (March 15, 2026):** Container at Bench 3 listed as "NaCl, 500g, Lot #KC-2024-0317" — consistent with label. No azide compound listed at Bench 3.
- **Separately labeled NaN₃ stock:** One (1) container of sodium azide, 100g, properly labeled in red, stored in Flammables Cabinet B. Quantity matches procurement records. This container is intact and correctly stored.
- **Discrepancy:** The mislabeled Bench 3 container does not correspond to any procurement or transfer record. It appears to be a **decanted portion of sodium azide placed into a sodium chloride container** after March 15, 2026 — the date of the last inventory update.

## Acid Reagents

- 1M Hydrochloric acid (HCl), 500 mL graduated cylinder, found at Bench 3 adjacent to stir plate. Approximately 50 mL missing. Consistent with use during an experiment.
- pH meter (Mettler Toledo S220) found powered on at Bench 3, last measurement recorded at 03:41 AM (internal clock).

## Summary

The evidence indicates that a sodium azide container was deliberately relabeled as sodium chloride and placed at Bench 3. The swap occurred after March 15, 2026. Yoon Jaehee's experiment protocol (see separate file) called for adding HCl to an NaCl sample drawn from the Bench 3 stock bottle.
