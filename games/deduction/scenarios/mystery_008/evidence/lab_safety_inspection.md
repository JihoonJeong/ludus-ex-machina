# Laboratory Safety Inspection Report
**Inspection Reference:** USO-INSP-2026-0328-B3
**Location:** Hanyang University, Chemistry Building 3, Laboratory 307 (Kim Lab)
**Inspection Date:** March 28, 2026
**Inspector:** Han Gyuseok, University Safety Office (Certified Chemical Safety Inspector, Grade 1)

---

## Summary

Routine semi-annual safety inspection of Laboratory 307. This is the second inspection cycle for 2025–2026 academic year. Findings below represent unresolved and newly identified violations. Prior inspection: January 15, 2026.

---

## Violations Identified

### Violation 1 — Chemical Storage: Secondary Containment
**Severity:** High
**Description:** Corrosive acid reagents (HCl, H₂SO₄, acetic acid) stored on Bench 2 open shelving without secondary containment trays. Secondary containment is required under University Safety Code §4.2.1 for all corrosive liquids > 100 mL.
**Responsible Party:** Lee Soyeon (Lab Technician — chemical storage responsibility per Lab SOP-307-02)
**Status:** Not corrected since January 2026 inspection. Formal warning issued.

### Violation 2 — Expired Safety Data Sheets (SDS)
**Severity:** Medium
**Description:** SDS documents for three reagents (sodium borohydride, dimethyl sulfoxide, and formaldehyde) expired over 12 months ago. Current SDS must be maintained per University Safety Code §2.1.4.
**Responsible Party:** Lee Soyeon (SDS management per Lab SOP-307-01)
**Status:** Not corrected since January 2026 inspection.

### Violation 3 — Personal Protective Equipment
**Severity:** Medium
**Description:** Two face shields in storage found cracked and non-functional. Replacement not logged in equipment records.
**Responsible Party:** Lee Soyeon
**Status:** New finding.

---
