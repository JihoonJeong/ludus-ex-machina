## Experiment Title
Salt Hydrolysis Kinetics Under Variable Acidic Conditions
(Chapter 5 supplementary data — not disputed material)

## Purpose
To measure the rate of pH change when HCl is added to a sodium chloride solution at varying concentrations. Control experiment for Chapter 5 ionic strength analysis.

## Materials

| Item | Quantity | Source |
|------|----------|--------|
| NaCl (Sodium Chloride) | 10 g | Bench 3 stock bottle |
| 1M HCl | 50 mL | Acid cabinet, Bench 3 |
| Distilled water | 200 mL | Central tap |
| pH meter | 1 unit | Bench 3 instrument rack |
| 500 mL beaker | 1 | Glassware cabinet |
| Magnetic stir bar + plate | 1 | Bench 3 |

## Procedure

1. Weigh 10 g NaCl from Bench 3 stock bottle. Transfer to 500 mL beaker.
2. Add 200 mL distilled water. Stir until dissolved (approx. 2 min).
3. Calibrate pH meter using pH 4.0 and 7.0 buffer solutions.
4. Begin adding 1M HCl at 5 mL/min using burette. Record pH every 60 seconds.
5. Continue until total 50 mL HCl added. Record final pH.
6. Repeat at 0°C (ice bath) and 37°C (water bath) for temperature comparison.
7. Plot pH vs. time and vs. HCl volume added.

## Safety Notes
*(Written by author)*
"NaCl + HCl — no hazardous reaction. Routine acidification. Standard gloves and safety glasses sufficient. Fume hood not required."
