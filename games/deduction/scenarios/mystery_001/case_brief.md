# Case Brief: The Midnight Collector

## Incident Summary

On the night of March 10, 2026, the painting *Moonrise Over Bukhansan* by the late artist Seo Youngho was stolen from Gallery Maru in Samcheong-dong, Seoul. The painting, valued at approximately ₩800 million, was the centerpiece of a private exhibition that evening.

The gallery owner, Mr. Lee Sangwoo, locked up at 10:30 PM after the last guest departed. When he returned at 8:00 AM the next morning, the painting was gone.

## Initial Assessment

Police have opened an investigation into the theft. The method of entry and means of removing the painting are under investigation.

## Suspects

Three individuals have been identified as persons of interest:

- **Suspect A (Ms. Han Jiyeon):** Gallery assistant at Gallery Maru.

- **Suspect B (Mr. Shin Wonjae):** Independent art dealer who attended the exhibition.

- **Suspect C (Dr. Park Minsoo):** Private art collector who attended the exhibition.
