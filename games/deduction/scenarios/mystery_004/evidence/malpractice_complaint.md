# Malpractice Complaint — Draft

**From:** Ms. Hwang Eunyoung (through Attorney Park Jinyoung, Park & Lee Associates)
**Against:** Dr. Choi Wonsik / Lumière Dermatology Clinic
**Date of draft:** March 10, 2026
**Status:** NOT YET FILED

---

## Summary of Complaint

On September 15, 2025, Ms. Hwang Eunyoung received a laser skin resurfacing treatment (Fraxel Dual) at Lumière Dermatology Clinic, performed by Dr. Choi Wonsik. Following the procedure, Ms. Hwang experienced:

- Persistent hyperpigmentation on the left cheek (ongoing as of March 2026)
- Scarring along the jawline (two areas, approximately 1cm each)
- Emotional distress and loss of social confidence

Ms. Hwang contends that Dr. Choi used an incorrect laser setting (energy level too high for her skin type) and failed to conduct a proper patch test. She seeks:

- **₩2 billion in damages** (medical costs, corrective procedures, emotional distress, reputational harm)
- Formal apology from the clinic
- Report to the Korean Medical Association

## Dr. Choi's Response (obtained during investigation)

"The complaint is without merit. The patient had a rare adverse reaction that occurs in approximately 2-3% of cases even with correct settings. I followed all protocols. I offered to provide corrective treatment at no cost, but Ms. Hwang refused and hired a lawyer instead. This has been very stressful for me and my practice. A formal complaint to the KMA could seriously damage my reputation."

## Current Status

- Draft complaint prepared by Attorney Park Jinyoung on March 10, 2026
- Ms. Hwang had a consultation with Dr. Choi on March 22 (phone call, 5 min) — the content is disputed. Dr. Choi says she was scheduling her next appointment. Attorney Park says she was giving Dr. Choi "a final chance to settle before filing."
- **The complaint was never filed** due to the victim's death on March 25.

## Investigator's Assessment

The malpractice dispute gives Dr. Choi a strong potential motive — ₩2 billion would threaten the clinic's survival, and a KMA report could end his career. Dr. Choi was visibly agitated during questioning about the complaint. His lawyer confirmed the settlement negotiations had "reached an impasse." The timing is notable: the complaint was due to be filed the week of Ms. Hwang's death.
