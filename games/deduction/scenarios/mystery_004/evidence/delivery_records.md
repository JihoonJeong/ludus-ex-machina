## Investigator's Note

Laboratory-grade KCl is significantly more concentrated and pure than aquarium supplements. The 500g quantity ordered would be sufficient to prepare multiple lethal doses for IV injection. Mr. Hwang's explanation for the purchase is not supported by physical evidence at his residence.
