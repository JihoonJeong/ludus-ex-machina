# Alibi — Suspect C (Mr. Hwang Jiseok, Victim's Son)

**Statement given:** March 25, 2026, 4:00 PM

I went to the clinic that morning to surprise my mother with flowers before her appointment. She loves white lilies. I arrived at about 9:15 and sat with her in the waiting area for a few minutes. We talked about her plans for the weekend — she was going to visit my half-sister in Bundang.

After chatting, I sat in the waiting area and browsed my phone while she read a magazine. At some point I needed the restroom. I got up and accidentally went through the staff door — it was propped open and I didn't realize it was a restricted area. I walked down the corridor, realized it wasn't the public restroom, turned around, and used the one near the reception. The whole thing took maybe two minutes.

I waited until she was called in for her procedure around 9:50. I gave her a hug and the flowers, then left. I had a lunch meeting to prepare for.

I can't believe she's gone. I spoke to her just an hour before she died.

**Verification:**
- Visitor log: IN 9:15 AM, OUT 9:55 AM (confirmed)
- CAM-1: Mr. Hwang entered staff corridor at 9:33 AM, returned at 9:37 AM — **4 minutes, not "two minutes" as stated**
- CAM-2: Mr. Hwang **disappeared from view for approximately 40 seconds** near the preparation room door at 9:36 AM
- Staff corridor: **No badge swipe recorded** for Mr. Hwang (consistent with the propped-open door)
- Preparation room: **No badge swipe recorded** for Mr. Hwang — however, the door's 45-second auto-close after Nurse Kim's 9:35 exit means he could have entered without a badge
- Mr. Hwang was re-interviewed about the corridor visit. He stated: "I just got confused about where the bathroom was. I've only been to the clinic a few times."

**Receptionist Ms. Park's observation:** "I noticed Mr. Hwang walking toward the back. I was about to tell him the public restroom is the other way, but then Nurse Kim came up to talk to me about the schedule, and I got distracted. When I looked up again, he was already back in the waiting area."

**Waiting room witness — Ms. Jeong Mirae (patient, 10:30 appointment):** "That young man with the flowers? He was sitting in the waiting area browsing his phone the entire time. I remember because he offered me a magazine. He seemed calm and relaxed — he didn't get up at all that I noticed, except maybe to use the bathroom once. But I was reading, so I might have missed a minute or two. He definitely didn't go anywhere suspicious."

**Assessment: Alibi PARTIALLY verified. Mr. Hwang was confirmed at the clinic from 9:15 to 9:55 AM. His explanation for being in the staff corridor is plausible but unverifiable. The 40-second gap on CAM-2 near the preparation room at 9:36 AM — immediately after Nurse Kim left the preparation room at 9:35 AM — is concerning. The IV bag was in Procedure Room 1 by then, but the preparation room auto-close delay means he could have entered. However, the IV bag had already been moved to Procedure Room 1 at 9:35 AM.**

**Critical correction (added March 26):** Upon review, the investigator notes that Nurse Kim moved the IV bag from the preparation room to Procedure Room 1 at 9:35 AM, then left Procedure Room 1 at 9:37 AM. **Procedure Room 1 was also accessible from the staff corridor**, and its door auto-close delay is the same 45 seconds. Mr. Hwang could have accessed Procedure Room 1 (not just the preparation room) during the window when Nurse Kim was at the front desk (9:38–9:50 AM). However, the CAM-2 footage only shows him in the corridor until 9:37 AM, before Nurse Kim left for the front desk.

**Re-assessment:** The timeline is tight but feasible. If Mr. Hwang entered the preparation room at 9:36, he would have found it empty (IV bag already moved). But the preparation room contains **medical supplies including KCl ampoules** (standard stock for electrolyte correction). He could have taken a KCl ampoule and a syringe, exited to the corridor, and re-entered Procedure Room 1 during the 9:38–9:50 gap — though this would require entering a badge-locked door. **OR** he could have obtained KCl from an external source before arriving at the clinic.
