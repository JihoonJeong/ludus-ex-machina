# Case Brief: The Last Prescription

## Incident Summary

On the morning of March 25, 2026, Ms. Hwang Eunyoung (52), a prominent real estate investor, died during a visit to Lumière Dermatology Clinic in Gangnam, Seoul. She was pronounced dead at 10:34 AM.

Ms. Hwang had been a regular client of the clinic for over three years.

## Initial Assessment

The Gangnam Police initially treated the death as a potential medical accident. However, the National Forensic Service examination raised concerns, and the case was reclassified as a homicide investigation.

## Suspects

Three individuals have been identified as persons of interest:

- **Suspect A (Dr. Choi Wonsik):** Owner and lead dermatologist of Lumière Clinic.

- **Suspect B (Nurse Kim Sooyeon):** Head nurse at the clinic for 5 years.

- **Suspect C (Mr. Hwang Jiseok):** A visitor to the clinic on the morning of the incident.
