# Case Brief: The Double Alibi

## Incident Summary

On the morning of March 22, 2026, at approximately 7:10 AM, Mr. Jung Taeyang (45), CEO of Hangang Development Corp., was found dead at Pine Valley Pension in Gapyeong, Gyeonggi Province.

Mr. Jung was attending an annual reunion dinner with four college friends. The group of five arrived at the pension on the afternoon of March 21. Mr. Jung was last seen alive at approximately 12:30 AM on March 22.

## Initial Assessment

Local police initially considered the death accidental. However, subsequent examination raised questions, and the case was referred to the Gyeonggi Provincial Police major crimes unit for further investigation.

## Suspects

The four surviving members of the reunion group:

- **Suspect A (Ms. Choi Eunji):** Attorney. She discovered the body.

- **Suspect B (Mr. Kim Hyunwoo):** Managing Director at a securities firm.

- **Suspect C (Ms. Seo Yuna):** Business owner in Bundang.

- **Suspect D (Mr. Park Taehyun):** Architect. Organized this year's reunion.

**Note:** A sixth friend, Mr. Lee Joonho, cancelled two days before the reunion due to a family emergency. He was in Busan at the time and is not considered a suspect.
