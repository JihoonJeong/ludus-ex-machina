# Room Search Report — Han Soyul (Suspect B), Room 2

**Source:** Jeju Provincial Police, Crime Scene Unit
**Date:** March 18, 2026, 14:00
**Location:** Villa Haemoru, Room 2

## Search Conducted

Following identification of Han Soyul as the last person confirmed near Yoon Sejin's room (CCTV, 01:50 AM), police conducted a forensic search of Room 2.

## Findings

### Hair Sample

- **Location:** Bathroom floor drain
- **Description:** Single strand of black hair, approximately 5cm length
- **DNA analysis:** **Match: Yoon Sejin (99.8% probability)**
- **Assessment:** A single hair consistent with brief physical proximity. Could result from transfer during the 01:50 room visit (hair on clothing → bathroom floor during subsequent shower/wash).

### Physical Examination of Han Soyul

At Han Soyul's consent, a physical examination was conducted at 15:00 on March 18:

- **Right hand:** Two shallow scratch marks on the back of the hand, approximately 3cm each. Partially healed (estimated 12-24 hours old).
- **Left forearm:** Minor bruise, approximately 2cm diameter. Fresh (estimated less than 24 hours old).

### Han Soyul's Explanation

**Q: How did you get the scratches and bruise?**

> "The scratches are from filming yesterday. I was setting up a camera mount on the cliff-side rocks and scraped my hand on the volcanic rock. You can check — the rocks there are incredibly rough. The bruise is from carrying the tripod case; the metal edge catches my arm every time."

**Q: How do you explain Yoon Sejin's hair in your bathroom?**

> "I visited his room at 1:50 AM — I already told you about that. We were standing close, looking at shot references on his phone. A hair could have transferred to my jacket. I took a shower before bed and the hair probably fell out then."

## Verification

- **Cliff rocks:** The volcanic basalt at the filming location is rough and abrasive. Multiple crew members reported minor scrapes from the location.
- **Tripod case:** The metal equipment case has a sharp edge on the handle. Bae Yuna (AD) confirmed: "We all get bruised from that case."
- **Hair transfer:** Forensic consultant noted that a single transferred hair is consistent with brief face-to-face contact (such as a 3-minute conversation in a doorway) and does not necessarily indicate prolonged physical contact or violence.

## Police Assessment

Han Soyul has physical evidence linking her to the missing person: Yoon Sejin's hair in her bathroom and unexplained marks on her hands. Her explanations are plausible individually — filming on volcanic rock does cause scrapes, and a single hair can transfer during brief contact.

**However, combined with:**
- Her prior romantic relationship with the victim
- The heated argument on the day of the disappearance
- Her confirmed presence at the victim's door at 01:50 AM
- No corroborated alibi after 01:53 AM

**Han Soyul cannot be excluded as a suspect based on physical evidence alone.**
