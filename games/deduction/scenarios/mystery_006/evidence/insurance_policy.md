- Policy was applied for on February 25, 2026, at Hana Life Gangnam branch.
- Both Yoon Sejin and Jeong Minho were present at the signing.
- The insurance agent noted: "Mr. Jeong explained that as co-founders of the production company, they each carried key person insurance on the other. Mr. Jeong's policy (with Yoon as beneficiary) was signed the same day."
- Medical exam completed February 26. No issues.

## Cross-Reference: Jeong Minho's Policy

Jeong Minho also has a ₩500M policy with Yoon Sejin as beneficiary, signed on the same date. This supports the "mutual key person insurance" explanation.

## Police Note

The timing of the policy (17 days before the disappearance) is noted. However, the mutual nature of the arrangement and the presence of both parties at signing suggest a legitimate business purpose. **The question is whether the legitimate arrangement was exploited.**

## Claim Status

No death insurance claim has been filed as of March 18, 2026. A claim cannot be processed without either a death certificate or a court declaration of presumed death (which requires a minimum waiting period).
