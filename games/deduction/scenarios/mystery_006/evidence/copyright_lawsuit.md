# Copyright Lawsuit — Yoon Sejin vs Jeong Minho

**Source:** Seoul Central District Court, Case No. 2025가합54821
**Status:** Ongoing (pre-trial discovery phase)

## Case Summary

- **Plaintiff:** Yoon Sejin (Director, missing person)
- **Defendant:** Jeong Minho (Suspect A, Producer)
- **Filed:** October 8, 2025
- **Subject:** Ownership of documentary footage and intellectual property rights

## Dispute

Yoon Sejin filed suit claiming sole creative ownership over the documentary series "Coast to Coast" (working title), which the production company had been developing since 2024. Yoon argues that Jeong Minho's role was purely financial and administrative, giving him no claim to the IP.

Jeong Minho countered that as co-CEO and executive producer, he holds 50% ownership of all company-produced content under the partnership agreement signed in 2021.

## Financial Stakes

- "Coast to Coast" has a pre-sale agreement with Netflix Korea for ₩1.2 billion (contingent on delivery by June 2026).
- If Yoon Sejin is declared sole owner, Jeong Minho loses his claim to approximately **₩600M** in revenue.
- If the court upholds the partnership agreement, both parties share equally.

## Police Note

The copyright dispute gives Jeong Minho a potential financial motive: with Yoon Sejin absent, the lawsuit would be suspended and Jeong would retain operational control of the company and the Netflix deal. **However, this motive applies to incapacitation or intimidation, not necessarily to killing — the Netflix deal requires the director to be alive to complete the documentary.**
