# Witness Statement — Argument Between Yoon Sejin and Han Soyul

**Source:** Bae Yuna (Suspect D), witness statement
**Date:** March 18, 2026, 11:45 AM

## Statement

> "On the second day of filming [March 17], around 4 PM, I witnessed a heated argument between Director Yoon and Soyul [Han Soyul] near the cliff shooting location.
>
> It started over the camera angles — Director Yoon wanted a wide shot from the western cliff edge, and Soyul said it was too dangerous and wouldn't look good. But it escalated quickly into something more personal.
>
> Director Yoon said something like 'you always question my creative decisions' and Soyul responded 'maybe if you listened to anyone else for once.' It got louder. Director Yoon said 'I should have never brought you on this project' and Soyul shot back 'the feeling is mutual.'
>
> It lasted maybe 5 minutes. Taesik [Oh Taesik] and I were standing nearby and it was uncomfortable. Eventually Soyul walked away and went back to the van. Director Yoon stood at the cliff edge for a while looking at the ocean.
>
> They seemed fine at dinner, though — sitting at opposite ends of the table but not openly hostile."

## Oh Taesik's Corroboration

Oh Taesik confirmed the argument occurred as described. He added:

> "It was intense but not unusual for film sets. Directors and DPs argue about shots all the time. I didn't think much of it."

## Police Assessment

The argument establishes recent interpersonal tension between Han Soyul and Yoon Sejin. Combined with their prior romantic relationship and Han Soyul's confirmed visit to Yoon's room at 01:50 AM, this creates a circumstantial pattern. However, no physical evidence links Han Soyul to the disappearance.
