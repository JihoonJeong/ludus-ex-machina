# Sexual Harassment Report — Filed by Bae Yuna Against Yoon Sejin

**Source:** Seoul Mapo Police Station, Case No. 2025-SMP-0912-23
**Filed:** September 12, 2025
**Status:** Investigation suspended (complainant withdrew cooperation)

## Complaint Summary

On September 12, 2025, Bae Yuna (Suspect D) filed a sexual harassment complaint against Yoon Sejin at the Seoul Mapo Police Station.

## Alleged Incidents

According to the complaint:
- **June 2025:** Unwanted physical contact (shoulder touching, arm grabbing) during after-work dinners on three occasions.
- **August 2025:** Inappropriate comments about her appearance during a team meeting. Specific remarks not detailed in the public filing.
- **September 8, 2025:** Yoon Sejin allegedly sent a late-night text message with personal content. Bae Yuna provided the text to police but requested it not be included in the public file.

## Investigation Status

- Police contacted Yoon Sejin on September 20, 2025. He denied the allegations.
- On October 5, 2025, Bae Yuna informed police she wished to withdraw from the investigation, stating: "I've decided to handle this privately."
- Investigation was formally suspended on October 15, 2025.

## Post-Complaint Relationship

Despite the complaint, Bae Yuna continued working as Assistant Director on the Jeju documentary project. When asked why:

> "I need the work. This industry is small. I filed the report so there would be a record if it happened again, but I can't afford to burn bridges. The Director [Yoon] has been more careful since I filed it."

## Colleague Awareness

- Oh Taesik: "I heard rumors about a complaint but never discussed it with Yuna directly."
- Han Soyul: "Yuna mentioned something happened but didn't go into details. She seemed okay working with him on this project."
- Jeong Minho: "I was not aware of any formal complaint until today."

## Police Assessment

The harassment report establishes a motive for Bae Yuna to harbor resentment toward Yoon Sejin. The withdrawal of cooperation could indicate either resolution or continued fear. Combined with her presence at the villa, this constitutes a potential motive. However, Bae Yuna's room (Room 5) is at the opposite end of the hallway from Room 3, and she appears on CCTV only once during the relevant period (23:42, getting water).
