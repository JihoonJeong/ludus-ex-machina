# Alibi Statement — Han Soyul (Suspect B)

**Source:** Witness statement, recorded by Jeju Provincial Police
**Date:** March 18, 2026, 11:15 AM

## Han Soyul's Statement

> "Yes, I went to Sejin's room around 1:50 AM. I wanted to talk to him about the next day's shooting — specifically the cliff-side scene we'd been disagreeing about. I'd been in my room going over the shot list and realized I needed to resolve it before tomorrow.

## Police Assessment
